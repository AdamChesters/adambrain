Software used

This project builds on the work of these open source communities.

Three.js
Browser rendering and camera controls.
dcm2niix
Converting selected DICOM stacks into usable 3D volumes.
pydicom and pylibjpeg
Reading the DICOM archive and checking decoded scan values.
FreeSurfer
Brain reconstruction and tissue estimates.
3D Slicer
Source-linked inspection and editable image and tissue layers.
SimpleITK
Image alignment and processing.
NumPy, SciPy and NiBabel
Volume calculations, filtering and neuroimaging file handling.
scikit-image and Pillow
Image processing, surface experiments and review images.

Earlier prototypes and reconstruction experiments

brain2print and NiiVue helped with early browser-based tissue previews. Blender was used for editable presentation models.

Offline reconstruction experiments used PyTorch, NiftyMIC, NeSVoR, UniRes and Spinal Cord Toolbox. Those experiments aren’t all part of this head viewer.
